## Search Console decision gates

- [ ] Weekly: query/page/country/device/search-appearance export.
- [ ] Rewrite title and description when a page has at least 500 impressions and CTR is below the page-position benchmark.
- [ ] Expand a collection when it earns impressions for adjacent queries but has fewer than five relevant recipes.
- [ ] Translate a recipe beyond Azerbaijani and English only after the target country produces sustained impressions or referrals.
- [ ] Remove or merge pages that remain indexed with zero impressions after 90 days and have no unique editorial value.

## North-star measurement

- Organic engaged sessions.
- Search-to-save rate.
- Recipe completion rate.
- 30-day returning organic users.
- Indexed pages with impressions.
- Branded query growth.
- Engaged referrals from `chatgpt.com`, Perplexity and other answer engines.

## Research signals used

- Google Year in Search 2025: strong recipe interest around honey, chicken, soup, vegetables and quick-use ingredients.
- Pinterest Predicts 2026: “Cabbage Crush”, tactile food aesthetics and longer-lived planning trends; Pinterest reports that its trends last nearly twice as long as trends elsewhere.
- Google Search 2026 guidance: non-commodity, first-hand information; strong image/video; visible text; internal links; and structured data that matches the page.
- Google Recipe guidance: complete Recipe markup, ItemList collection pages, crawlable images and multiple high-resolution aspect ratios when available.

The strategy does not copy high-competition viral recipes. It bridges those broad appetites to Chef İlhamə’s defensible niche: searchable regional foods with limited high-quality coverage.
